A complete story in exactly six words: "She answered her own ransom note." A ransom note arrives, she answers it — and the note is hers. Who was taken, why she wrote it, and what she says back are left for the reader to fill in.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Running it writes the six-word story to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after validating it is exactly six words on one line.
